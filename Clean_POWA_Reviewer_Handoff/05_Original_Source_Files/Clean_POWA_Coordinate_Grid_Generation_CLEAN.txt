Completed the full coordinate grid using the established structure of 10 × 10 panels, with each panel containing 4 × 4 tiles, producing a 40 × 40 tile map and 1,600 total tile coordinates.

Under the T(column,row) format, the final coordinate is:

P(9,9)T(39,39)

T(99) would work as a linear tile index, but not as a column-and-row coordinate. Linear indices from 0–1599 are included in the workbook as a separate field.


The workbook contains:

- Coordinate Grid: the complete visual 40 × 40 map
- Coordinate List: all 1,600 coordinates with panel, global tile, local tile, and linear-index values
- Coordinate Rules: formulas, examples, and endpoint clarification
